02 | PROBLEM & HACKATHON ALIGNMENT

The Transit Anxiety & Information Vacuum in Jammu

Millions of pilgrims arrive at Jammu Tawi & Airport facing fear, exploitation, and zero curated guidance.

🚨 Transit Anxiety & Time Fear

Fear of missing onward trains or flights:

  • Pilgrims with 4 to 8 hour layovers remain stranded on station platforms.
  • Zero existing tools calculate safe return buffers through mountain bottlenecks.
  • High psychological stress leads travelers to avoid local sightseeing entirely.

💸 Tariff Gouging & Haggling

Unregulated local transit fares:

  • Unsuspecting families charged ₹800–₹1,500 for a ₹150 ride from station to city.
  • Matador mini-bus networks are opaque and undocumented for visitors.
  • Lack of official union price benchmarks causes constant friction and arguments.

📴 Mountain Dead Zones & Strain

Cellular blackouts & physical hardship:

  • Heavy 4G dropouts in Shivalik & Pir Panjal foothills crash cloud apps.
  • Senior yatris face unexpected steep stairs and lack battery-car info.
  • Emergency 112 and local medical help coordinates are scattered or unavailable offline.

04 | THE JAMMUKHOJ SOLUTION

An Intelligent, Resilient Local Guide Built for Pilgrims

A human-centered progressive platform engineered specifically for zero-data environments and time-bound travelers.

⚡ 1-Tap Buffer-Safe Circuit Engine

Deterministic layout generator tailored to traveler's exact layover window (4h Express, 8h Cultural, 24h Valley trail). Enforces a strict 45-minute train buffer and 150-minute flight buffer, preventing any possibility of missed departures.

📶 100% Offline Vector Architecture

Built on IndexedDB (Dexie) with bundled vector maps and 36+ curated attractions. Operates flawlessly without an active SIM or data roaming in mountain valleys and railway yards.

🚌 Transparent Transit Intelligence

Verified route maps and stage-by-stage tariff benchmarks for Matador mini-buses (₹10–25), Smart City E-Buses, Auto-Rickshaws, and Airport/Station Prepaid Taxis.

🧓 Elder & Pilgrim Care

Step-free badges, walking load indicators (Low/Med/High), verified Vaishno Devi 5-stage trek manual, battery-car regulations, and 1-tap ERSS 112 WhatsApp location SOS.
