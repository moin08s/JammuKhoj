02 • PROBLEM & HACKATHON ALIGNMENT

The Transit Anxiety & Information Vacuum in Jammu

Millions of pilgrims arrive at Jammu Tawi & Airport facing fear, exploitation, and zero curated guidance.

🚨 Transit Anxiety & Time Fear

Fear of missing onward trains / flights:

  • Pilgrims face 4 to 12 hour layovers at Jammu Tawi or IXJ Airport.
  • Fear of mountain highway bottlenecks traps families on railway platforms.
  • Zero existing tools compute guaranteed conservative safety return buffers.

💸 Transport Gouging & Haggling

Unregulated local transit fares:

  • Unsuspecting yatris routinely charged ₹800–₹1,500 for a ₹150 taxi ride.
  • Matador mini-bus stages and route numbers are completely opaque to outsiders.
  • Zero visibility into official prepaid auto/taxi union rate cards.

📴 Mountain Dead Zones & Strain

Cellular blackouts & physical hardship:

  • Cloud-based travel apps crash in Shivalik foothill tunnels and dead zones.
  • Senior citizens face unexpected steep stairs without wheelchair/battery-car guidance.
  • Emergency hotlines and National ERSS 112 are scattered across fragmented websites.

05 • USER JOURNEY

From Station Arrival to Holy Darshan in 5 Steps

Frictionless end-to-end pilgrimage experience without forced registration or learning curves.

STEP 1: ARRIVAL AT STATION / AIRPORT

Yatri steps off train at Jammu Tawi (JAT) or IXJ Airport. Opens jammukhoj.in directly with 1 tap. Zero mandatory login barrier.

STEP 2: 1-TAP ITINERARY GENERATION

Selects layover: '4-Hour Express' from station. App automatically reserves 45-min departure buffer and crafts a 2.5-hr curated circuit.

STEP 3: TRANSPARENT LOCAL TRANSIT

Checks Matador Route #1 to Raghunath Bazaar. Boards with exact ₹15 fare; avoids cab touts quoting ₹400 for the same distance.

STEP 4: CULTURAL DISCOVERY & PURE VEG FOOD

Offers prayers at Raghunath Mandir, tastes authentic Kaladi Kulcha with Jain/Pure Veg filter, and visits verified Basohli craft shop.

STEP 5: SAFE ONWARD DEPARTURE

Returns to station with 50 minutes to spare. Boards onward Vande Bharat to Katra for holy darshan with complete peace of mind.
